    A major focus of the growth mindset in schools is coaxing students away from seeing failure as an indication of their ability, and towards seeing it as a chance to improve that ability. (学校推行成长型思维的核心重点，是引导学生不再将失败视为自身能力的体现，而是把它当作提升能力的契机。)

  • instill /ɪnˈstɪl/ vt. 徐徐滴入；逐渐灌输

    instilled 灌输

  • former /ˈfɔːmə(r)/ adj.（两者中）前者的

    the former took on only those tasks that would not risk their sense of worth. 前者只会承担那些对自身价值没有威胁的任务；

  • inferred /ɪnˈfɜːd/ v. 推测（infer 的过去式和过去分词）

    and adapting her work accordingly; 根据她的工作进行相应调整

    being conflated with … 与…. 混为一谈；
