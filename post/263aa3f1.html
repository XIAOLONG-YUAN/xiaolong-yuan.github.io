优化后

Nowadays, online shopping has become an increasingly popular way of shopping, while traditional in-store shopping is still a common choice for many people. In my opinion, online shopping is a better choice, and its advantages are more obvious than those of offline shopping.

First of all, online shopping is extremely convenient for modern people. For example, people do not need to go out of their homes, and they can shop conveniently at any time by using a mobile phone, which saves a lot of precious time, especially for busy office workers.

Besides, online shopping is more economical. For instance, related service providers usually have lower costs. What is more, people can easily compare different options with just a few simple operations, which helps them make better choices.

Finally, online shopping offers great personalization. E-commerce platforms also allow for a highly personalized and customized user experience through technologies and data analytics. Data about a customer’s past purchases and online activities offer insight into their interests, which can be used to deliver a personalized experience.

offer insight into sth 「提供关于某事的深入了解；洞悉某事」
a personalized experience 「个性化体验」
deliver （v. 提供）

Although offline shopping allows people to check products in person, this small advantage cannot match the benefits of online shopping. In conclusion, online shopping is more convenient, economical, and personalized. For these reasons, I definitely support online shopping as a superior choice for most people.

in person (亲自)
superior （adj. （规模或实力）更大的，更强的，(在品质上)更好的；）
