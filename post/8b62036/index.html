speaking-art

Part 1

  • Do you like art?

    To be honest, I don’t really like art that much. I don’t know much about it, and it’s hard for me to understand these abstract pieces. I do respect artists for their creativity, but I’d rather spend my free time on music or sports. That said, I still enjoy visiting art exhibitions occasionally, especially if they’re interactive or have a clear theme.

    说实话，我不太喜欢艺术。我对它了解不多，有时觉得抽象作品很难理解。我确实很尊重艺术家的创造力，但我更愿意把空闲时间花在音乐或运动上。话虽如此，我偶尔还是喜欢参观艺术展览，尤其是那些互动性强或者主题明确的展览。

    eg: I sometimes find abstract pieces confusing

    • It’s hard for me to understand these abstract pieces.
    • These abstract pieces are confusing to me.
  • Do you like visiting art galleries?

    Not really. To be honest, I don’t usually go to art galleries because I find them a bit boring. I’d rather do something more active or fun, like going to a concert or walking around an outdoor market. I know some people love art, but it just doesn’t speak to me—I can appreciate it, but I don’t feel any strong connection.

    不太喜欢。说实话，我通常不去美术馆，因为我觉得那里有点无聊。我宁愿去做些更活跃或更有趣的事情，比如去听音乐会或者逛户外市场。我知道有些人很喜欢艺术，但对我来说没什么吸引力——我能欣赏它，但感觉不到强烈的共鸣。

  • Do you want to be an artist?

    I wouldn’t want to be a professional artist, but I’d really like to keep making art as a hobby. It helps me relax after a busy day, and I find things like sketching or doing crafts very calming. So even though I won’t make it my career, I think art will always be an important part of my personal life.

    我不想成为职业艺术家，但我真的很愿意把创作艺术当作一种爱好。它能帮我放松，尤其是在忙碌一天之后，我觉得素描或做手工非常让人平静。所以即使我不会把它当作职业，我也觉得艺术会一直是我个人生活中重要的一部分。

  • Do you like modern art or traditional art?

    I actually like modern art more because it’s bold and experimental, and it often talks about today’s social issues. Modern installations or abstract works make me think and understand things in my own way. I also think it’s exciting when artists break old rules and challenge what we usually think of as ‘art’.

    我其实更喜欢现代艺术，因为它大胆、富有实验性，而且常常探讨当今的社会问题。现代装置艺术或抽象作品会让我用自己的方式去思考和理解事物。我也觉得艺术家打破旧规则、挑战我们对“艺术”的常规看法，这很令人兴奋。

Part 2

  • Describe a person who met difficulties but succeeded You should say:
    1. Who this person is
    2. What difficulties he met
    3. How he overcame the difficulties And explain how you feel about him

    My High School Teacher 我的高中老师

    I’d like to talk about my high school physics teacher. He grew up in a very remote village where there were almost no good schools or learning materials. Because of that, he didn’t have many chances to study well when he was young. He took the college entrance exam twice but failed both times. However, he didn’t give up. He kept studying by himself using old textbooks and borrowed notes. Finally, on his third try, he got accepted into one of the top universities in the country. I really admire him because he never lost hope, even when things looked really bad.

    我想说说我高中的物理老师。他成长在一个非常偏远的村庄，那里几乎没有好学校或学习资料。因此，他年轻时没有太多机会好好学习。他参加了两次高考，但都失败了。然而，他没有放弃，而是用旧课本和借来的笔记坚持自学。最终，在第三次尝试时，他被国内一所顶尖大学录取了。我真的很钦佩他，因为即使在情况看起来很糟糕的时候，他也从未失去希望。

Part 3

  • In your country(国家), what industry is it easier to be successful in?

    **Tech Industry **科技行业

    I think it’s easier to be successful in the tech industry in my country. Because now everything is going digital, so there are more chances. Many young people start small companies in areas like AI or app development, and they don’t need a lot of money at first. Also, the government helps a lot, and many businesses need tech solutions.

    我觉得在我国家，科技行业更容易取得成功。因为现在一切都在数字化，所以机会更多。很多年轻人在人工智能或应用程序开发等领域创办小公司，一开始也不需要太多钱。而且政府支持力度很大，很多企业都需要科技解决方案。

  • What’s the difference between ordinary people and successful people?

    Attitude Toward Failure对失败的态度

    Successful people don’t see failure as the end. They think of it as useful feedback—something that shows them what to improve. Ordinary people might feel embarrassed or lose confidence after failing, but successful ones look at what went wrong and try again. This helps them use problems as steps toward success.

    成功人士不把失败看作终点。他们认为失败是有用的反馈——能告诉他们哪里需要改进。普通人失败后可能会觉得尴尬或失去信心，但成功人士会分析哪里出错了然后再次尝试。这帮助他们把问题变成通往成功的台阶。

  • What are the factors leading to people’s success?

    Clear Goals and Planning明确目标与规划

    Successful people usually know exactly what they want and make plans to get there. They set clear goals and break them into small steps so it’s easier to follow. They also change their plans if something isn’t working. Without a good plan, even very talented people might get lost and not reach their full potential.

    成功的人通常很清楚自己想要什么，并制定计划去实现。他们会设定明确的目标，并将其分解成小步骤，这样更容易执行。如果某些方法行不通，他们也会调整计划。如果没有一个好的计划，即使非常有才华的人也可能迷失方向，无法发挥全部潜力。
